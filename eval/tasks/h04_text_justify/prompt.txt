Write a function justify(words, width) that lays out a list of words as fully justified lines,
each exactly width characters long, and returns the list of lines.
- Pack words greedily: put as many words on each line as fit with at least one space between them.
- Spread the extra spaces between the words as evenly as possible. If they don't divide evenly,
  the gaps on the left get one more space than the gaps on the right.
- The last line is left-justified: words separated by single spaces, padded with spaces on the
  right to the full width. A line with only one word is also left-justified that way.
- An empty list of words returns []. Raise ValueError if any word is longer than width.
For example justify(["This", "is", "an", "example", "of", "text", "justification."], 16) returns
["This    is    an", "example  of text", "justification.  "].
